    She doesn't walk into a room so much as arrive in one. The air gets softer, the light gets interested, and I get nervous — in the best possible way.

Her Hearteven more beautiful

Because the truth is — the face was only the cover, and the cover made me pick up the book. But the heart? The heart is the whole story:

  1. no. 01

    Her kindness

    She gives it away like it costs her nothing, and somehow wakes up with more. I've watched her hand her umbrella to a stranger and walk home smiling in the rain.

  2. no. 02

    Her strength

    Soft enough to hold everyone she loves, strong enough to carry them on the days they can't walk. Gentle and unbreakable — I didn't know a person could be both.

  3. no. 03

    Her soul

    An old, warm soul. The kind that feels like a song you've known your whole life, hummed by someone you've only just met.

  4. no. 04

    Her love

    Once she gives it to you, you finally understand what every song, every poem, and every love story in history was so worked up about.
